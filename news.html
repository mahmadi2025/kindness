2026-07-26

Field Activity & Transparency Report: AFN 41,200 Distributed in Direct Aid

We are grateful to report the successful completion of our latest direct humanitarian assistance initiative in Afghanistan. Totaling AFN 41,200 in donor funds, assistance was deployed directly on the ground to address urgent, life-threatening needs:

  • Medical Assistance (AFN 12,000): Financial aid provided to a female beneficiary to cover part of the cost of a critical surgical operation at a local hospital.
  • Emergency Food Relief (AFN 23,220): Essential food packages purchased and distributed directly to six vulnerable families to meet their immediate nutritional needs and alleviate hardship.
  • Essential Medicines (AFN 5,470): Life-essential prescription medicines purchased and provided to two vulnerable patients requiring urgent medical treatment.
  • Transportation and Delivery (AFN 510): Direct transport costs incurred to deliver food assistance directly to the recipient families.

Governance & Transparency: All expenditures were carried out under our formal agency agreement and are fully supported by vendor receipts, beneficiary lists with thumbprints, and field video documentation. Thank you to our generous donors for making this life-changing assistance possible!
