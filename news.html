Financial Reconciliation 2026-08-30

Kabul Aid Remittance Audit: AFN 122,700 ($2,836 CAD) Reconciled to TD Bank

In accordance with our commitment to absolute financial transparency and CRA direction-and-control standards, we are pleased to report that cumulative humanitarian aid transferred to Kabul has reached AFN 122,700 ($2,836 CAD) across three direct relief missions:

  • Spring 2026 Mission (TD Debit $912 CAD): AFN 40,000 remitted and deployed directly for emergency family food relief.
  • Summer 2026 Mission (TD Debit $962 CAD): AFN 41,200 deployed for a critical female patient surgery (AFN 12,000), food hampers for 6 families (AFN 23,220), and prescription medicines (AFN 5,470).
  • Late Summer 2026 Mission (TD Debit $962 CAD): AFN 41,500 remitted and deployed for urgent family sustenance and medical relief.

TD Bank & MoneyGram Three-Way Audit Match: Every single remittance (Apr 20: $912.00, May 20: $962.00, Aug 24: $962.00) matches our official Canadian TD Bank statement debits to the exact penny, with 0% platform or administrative fee deductions. Thank you to our donors for making this direct impact possible!

Field Report 2026-07-26

Field Activity & Transparency Report: AFN 41,200 Distributed in Direct Aid

We are grateful to report the successful completion of our latest direct humanitarian assistance initiative in Afghanistan. Totaling AFN 41,200 in donor funds, assistance was deployed directly on the ground to address urgent, life-threatening needs:

  • Medical Assistance (AFN 12,000): Financial aid provided to a female beneficiary to cover part of the cost of a critical surgical operation at a local hospital.
  • Emergency Food Relief (AFN 23,220): Essential food packages purchased and distributed directly to six vulnerable families to meet their immediate nutritional needs and alleviate hardship.
  • Essential Medicines (AFN 5,470): Life-essential prescription medicines purchased and provided to two vulnerable patients requiring urgent medical treatment.
  • Transportation and Delivery (AFN 510): Direct transport costs incurred to deliver food assistance directly to recipient families.

Governance & Transparency Verification: All expenditures were carried out under our formal CRA-compliant Agency Agreement and are 100% supported by itemized vendor receipts, beneficiary distribution sign-off lists with thumbprints, and field video documentation. Thank you to our generous donors for making this direct assistance possible!

Winter Relief 2025-10-15

Winter Distribution Campaign Begins in Kabul

As winter temperatures approach sub-zero levels in Kabul, Kindness for Afghans has initiated its 2025 Winter Relief distribution. Thanks to your generous support, warm clothing, heavy blankets, and heating fuels are being delivered directly to 50 vulnerable families, widows, and orphans facing extreme seasonal hardship.

Milestone 2025-05-20

Official Launch of Kindness for Afghans

We are proud to announce the official launch and incorporation of Kindness for Afghans as a registered Ontario Not-for-Profit Corporation (OCN: 1001294785). Founded by dedicated volunteers in Canada, our mission is to eliminate administrative overhead and deliver 100% of charitable donations directly into the hands of those who need it most.
