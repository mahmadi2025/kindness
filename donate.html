Canadian Governance & Transparency Notice

Kindness for Afghans is incorporated as an Ontario Not-for-Profit Corporation (OCN: 1001294785). We are currently operating as a non-profit while our federal registered charity application with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is under processing. While this means official Canadian tax receipts cannot be issued until registration is finalized, 100% of your donation is deployed directly to emergency humanitarian aid without any deductions for administrative salaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our direct aid delivery and governance.

How do you ensure 100% of my donation reaches those in need?
Kindness for Afghans is 100% volunteer-run by professionals in Canada. We have no executive salaries or paid marketing campaigns. All field aid is executed under a formal Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Agency Agreement with strict three-way reconciliation: every dollar is matched against itemized vendor receipts, recipient distribution lists with thumbprints, and field documentation.
How do funds safely reach Afghanistan under banking restrictions?
Due to international commercial banking sanctions on Afghanistan, standard Canadian bank wire transfers into Kabul are restricted. We deploy authorized remittance channels (including MoneyGram via Canadian financial counters) directly to our vetted field representative under our legal agency agreement. Full bank debit statements, MoneyGram remittance receipts, and local disbursement reports are reconciled to the penny.
How are beneficiary families selected?
Our on-the-ground representatives work directly within communities to identify families facing acute, life-threatening hardship—giving strict priority to widows with young children, orphan-headed households, disabled individuals, and families with zero income or breadwinners.
Are there any hidden platform processing fees?
None! We deliberately chose Zeffy for online credit/debit card donations because Zeffy charges 0% transaction fees and 0% platform fees to non-profits (funded entirely by voluntary donor tips). For Canadian donors, Interac e-Transfer to info@kindnessforafghans.org is also 100% free of processing deductions.
Can I donate from outside Canada?
Yes! Our Zeffy online payment portal accepts international credit and debit cards from donors in the United States, Europe, Australia, and worldwide. For large wire transfers, please email us directly at info@kindnessforafghans.org for wire routing instructions.
