How We Work

A direct, accountable humanitarian model that eliminates middleman friction.

1

Community Identification

Our trusted local field coordinators assess communities directly, identifying families in acute distress—prioritizing widows, orphans, and households without income earners.

2

Zero-Middleman Delivery

Relief supplies (food staples, pharmacy prescriptions, and winter fuels) are purchased directly from local vendors and hand-delivered directly to recipient households.

3

Full Audit Verification

Under our CRA-compliant Agency Agreement, every expenditure is documented with itemized vendor receipts, recipient sign-off sheets with thumbprints, and field documentation.

Our Mission & Governance

At Kindness for Afghans, our mission is to restore dignity, hope, and relief to the most vulnerable families in Afghanistan. We collect charitable contributions from Canada, the United States, and internationally to deliver immediate, life-sustaining aid.

Kindness for Afghans is an incorporated Ontario Not-for-Profit Corporation (Ontario Corporation Number: 1001294785). We operate with complete financial transparency, maintaining formal agency agreements that satisfy Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) overseas direction-and-control standards.
